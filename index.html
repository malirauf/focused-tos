What the App Does

Focused lets you define geographic "zones" and a list of apps to block while you're physically inside an active zone. Blocking is enforced using Android's Accessibility Service and location/geofencing, and is intended to help you stay focused — not as a parental-control, enterprise-device-management, or security tool.

Blocking is best-effort, not guaranteed. Location accuracy, Android's background-process limits, battery optimization settings, device manufacturer restrictions, and permissions you've granted or revoked can all affect whether a zone is detected or an app is actually blocked in time. Don't rely on Focused in situations where a missed or delayed block could cause you harm (for example, safety-critical settings).
